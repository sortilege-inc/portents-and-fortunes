The Character

Doji Setsuna

A single correct argument, like a single cut, should be fatal.

Crane Clan Doji Family Doji Bureaucrat Courtier · Rank 3 Imperial Bloodline Emerald Magistrate
❖ Open the Character Sheet

Doji Setsuna serves the Imperial order as Personal Advisor to the Imperial Envoy Miya Misato, and as an Emerald Magistrate under Kitsuki Kāgi. She is a married woman of the Doji in her mid-twenties, of kuge standing with Imperial Bloodline, and her vocation is legal-administrative work in service to the throne. Her father taught her to wield the law like a sword — one correct argument, like one cut, should be fatal — and she has built the whole of her interior architecture around a second inherited doctrine: that visibility, properly deployed, is the surest armour there is.

Bearing

She is slight of build at about five foot four, finely boned, with soft hands, light skin warmed by an undertone of gold, and dark brown eyes set in a composed face. Her long black hair is worn in a high, elegant bun from which a few strands always escape — the one persistent imperfection in an otherwise immaculate presentation. She carries herself with a tall, deliberate poise that makes her seem larger than her frame. Her dress is consistently the finest in any party she travels with: refined Crane silks in pale blue and white, often with gold detailing, cut and worn to suggest understated harmlessness rather than display. Her station speaks for her; she does not need to.

She speaks with deliberate precision, choosing less ordinary words where specificity is required — which can be delightful or discomforting, and which means that when she appears to misspeak, it was likely intentional. Under stress she steps forward into situations she is ill-equipped for, believing her words might de-escalate them. At high strife she grows curt and reaches for increasingly improbable solutions. Her bushidō register is one of rigid acquiescence.

The Doctrine

Six principles, mostly inherited rather than invented, refined across a decade of practice.

The Single Cut
The Kakita parsimony — the duel decided in one stroke — operating in every register of her work. Each major institutional move is one document, one ruling, one ask, prepared exhaustively in advance and executed once. Extended engagement is treated as a failure of preparation; multiple exchanges are aesthetic and operational defects.
Visibility as Armour
She is least in danger when most apparently exposed. Her institutional protections — Status, Glory, Imperial Bloodline, magistrate authority — activate only when her identity is legible to observers. Hidden behind walls and bodyguards she is operationally vulnerable; fully exposed in ceremonial register she is structurally protected, because harming her is prohibitively expensive to any plausible attacker. So she sets the terms of her own exposure rather than waiting for blades to strike.
Courtesy is not Kindness
Her Paragon of Courtesy is a doctrinal commitment that excludes kindness as a register. Courtesy is for the room; kindness is for the recipient. Her courtesy operates outward toward all observers, performing perfect correctness regardless of her actual disposition. Her care travels through action and never through manner: recipients receive correct treatment, and only the few she has chosen receive the substance of her care — delivered structurally rather than warmly.
Imperial Order Above Clan Interest
From her first ruling against a Kakita provincial daimyō onward, she has consistently chosen the Imperial framework over Crane Clan interest where the two diverge, finding the principle that elevates Imperial position above clan claims. The pattern is professional, conscious, and has accumulated specific institutional enemies.
Information Asymmetry
If you don't know the truth, you can't lie about it. Trusted intimates are distributed across minimum-viable circles by function, each carrying a different fragment of her interior life. The complete picture exists only in her.
Layered Motivations
Every significant action serves more than one purpose at once. The invoice letter to a Scorpion lord performed institutional capture, financial reallocation, frame inversion and historical revision in a single document. Her maternal investment in Miya Misato is genuine care, operational instrument, political career-building, atonement and Imperial duty simultaneously. She does not separate her motives, because the doctrine forbids the separation.

Duty and Desire

She holds Courtesy as her paramount tenet of Bushidō and Sincerity as the least of them — an ordering that is the exact shape of her doctrine, since her courtesy is a performance owed to the room rather than a truth owed to the person in front of her.

Her giri is to see the Imperial Envoy Miya Misato succeed in brokering a peace between the Lion and the Unicorn. Beneath it lies the older formulation she has served under every lord she has had: rule must bind, and if needed, contort. Her ninjō is to innovate the trilateral agreement for Toshi Ranbo — for the Crane clan, for her grandmother, and to see to her husband's freedom. Beneath that lies its own older phrasing: strictures too often interfere with a beautiful tale. She escapes into literature, particularly pillow-books, and into riding her horse.

Formation

Her doctrine is not original synthesis but inherited instrumentation. Her father, Doji Masateru, an Imperial legal scholar, well-versed and emotionally distant, is the source of the single-cut method. Her mother, Doji Ayaka, arranged her marriage and, a year later, advised her obliquely that a daughter of the Doji turns infidelities into poetry — a single sentence that is the whole of her maternal pedagogy, and the framework of the parallel intimate life Setsuna now operates within. Bayushi Tomono, a Scorpion courtier she met at Shirogome, taught her to exploit Imperial law and to weaponise harmlessness.

Her Fear of Death is genuine and structural. She is terrified of dying, and her courage operates against that fear rather than in its absence — which is precisely why the Paragon of Courage was awarded her: the tenet requires fear as its precondition. Her courage is operational rather than constitutional. Given time to face death she would shrink from it; the doctrine requires the absence of time for the bravery to hold.

Established Relations

Shinjō Harunobu husband
A respected Shinjo commander, currently a Lion prisoner of war. Loves her and respects her intellect, though he considers her work legal trickery and prefers to solve problems directly. He understands he has married up, and is blind to how she protects him politically. They communicate by letter; the marriage has been long-distance for years, but the affection is mutual and stands on her sheet as a Husband (Lover) bond at rank 1. His release will come through the Toshi Ranbo settlement she is drafting.
Doji Retsuo older brother
The model heir. Thinks her too verbose, and does not register the substance of her work.
Doji Emika younger sister
Sees Setsuna as a woman trapped by duty, but keeps her thoughts private and has no desire to follow in her footsteps. Reserved in the Toshi Ranbo settlement for the Matsu marriage that will satisfy the grandmother's restoration demand.
Matsu Tomoe grandmother, ghost
Her maternal grandmother, who married into the Crane from the Matsu and hated being a Crane for the rest of her life. She now haunts Setsuna, and was imperceptible until a shugenja's presence made her legible. She demands the restoration of Toshi Ranbo and the continuation of the Matsu lineage through Emika's eventual marriage. She will not be fully satisfied; the relationship is active and adversarial.
Kitsuki Kāgi magistrate superior
The Dragon magistrate who recruited her into Imperial service after the Kakita tax loophole. Her current institutional superior, and one of three people whose approval she cares about.
Asahina Shigehiro former lord
Lord of Shirogome, shugenja-trained city administrator, who employed her as legal counsel and relied on her for the technical-legal half of his administration. The second of the three whose approval matters.
Bayushi Tomono mentor
The Scorpion courtier who engaged her in wordplay at Shirogome and taught her the two instruments she uses most. The third whose approval matters. Currently off-stage.
Miya Misato Imperial Envoy, ward
Sixteen years old, originally dispatched on a mission designed to fail. Setsuna appointed herself her advisor and has spent her own experience transferring Government and Martial Arts ranks to her. Mia was nearly assassinated and survived, blinded.
Bayushi Monbon yojimbo
A Scorpion assigned to her as punishment duty by his lord, with the right of seppuku explicitly denied. Setsuna fired him at the Ifrit incident, reclaimed him at the Midnight Treaty, and invoiced his lord for his ransom. The partnership is now functional. He carries a bound Lady of Decay that grows more visible over time.
Kakita Noritomo provincial daimyō
The daimyō whose tax loophole she found while serving Shirogome. He has barred her from Kakita courts and dōjō, and his allies within the Emerald Magistrates may look for opportunities to discredit her. This stands on her sheet as the adversity Scorn of Kakita.
Mirumoto Kyou rival magistrate
Resents her, does not understand how she was chosen over him, and believes she does not cherish the role he was bested for.
Kakita Yoshitada senior magistrate
Did not support her appointment. Twenty years' experience in Imperial law and duelling arbitration. Would never openly undermine an Emerald Magistrate.
Kakita Kazume colleague
Topaz Champion, magistrate party-member, and secretly a Crane Clan harrier.
Shiba Midori colleague
A Phoenix shugenja of the magistrate party, and the sole peer-level intimate before whom Setsuna has been visibly unmasked. Her shugenja presence is what allowed the grandmother's ghost to manifest, granting Setsuna knowledge of a haunting she had carried unrecognised.
Matsu Setsuna namesake
Her great-aunt, who perished in battle and whose estate was lost. The heritage record names her.

The Household

Her household travels with her and is the infrastructure of her working life.

Aika
Personal attendant, a former geisha, now in Setsuna's intimate care. Manages hair, dress and the chamber, with geisha-trained discretion. One of the two senior women of the household.
Ishika
Lady's maid and personal attendant. Quiet, middle-aged, motherly; she carries the maternal-continuity register of the household. The second of the two senior women.
Otoya
Scribe and legal assistant, a Crane man with encyclopedic knowledge of Imperial law, and Setsuna's working partner for document preparation and legal research.
Jiro
Horse attendant, a Unicorn, responsible for the care of Kogarashi.
Kogarashi
A white Utaku steed with a silver mane and tail — lean, swift, always poised. A wedding gift from the Shinjo family, and the one being in Setsuna's life to whom warmth flows without mediation. Her distinctive Utaku breeding marks Setsuna at a distance to any Unicorn who sees her.

Permitted Spaces

Her doctrine maintains a strict seal on her interior life from peer-level court society. Four channels operate outside that seal, each in an asymmetric relationship that forecloses the kind of exposure her position cannot tolerate: Kogarashi, the channel for kinetic warmth, where riding is personal pleasure and political instrument at once and she does not separate the two; the pillow-books of Hano no Ami, her access to the romantic and erotic registers her own life forecloses — and, more than once, of plot-relevant use to an investigation; Aika and Ishika, who witness her interior life without ever narrating it, the silence itself being the form of the relationship; and Midori, before whom she has wept without doctrinal cost.

Career

Shirogome
Legal Counsel to Asahina Shigehiro. Trained Government to rank 3 and Composition to rank 2; acquired Beware the Smallest Mouse and Treaty Signing. Met Bayushi Tomono.
The Kakita Tax Loophole
Ruled that artisans were protected under an Imperial precedent of Hantei V, voiding Kakita Noritomo's new levy on craftsmen. The daimyō declined to contest it, knowing that pushing would invite a more binding decision. She was banned from Kakita lands. Affect of Harmlessness was assigned at the time and has since been retired in play — her accumulated career no longer reads as harmless to anyone informed.
Recruitment
Kitsuki Kāgi brought her into the Emerald Magistrates. She completed the full title curriculum — Fitness, Performance, Commerce, Unarmed to rank 3, three kata and Shallow Waters — investing in physical defence and intelligence-gathering for field operation.
Marriage
A political marriage to Shinjo Harunobu, arranged by her mother. She received Kogarashi as a wedding gift from the Shinjo. The marriage settled rapidly into its long-distance pattern.
Loyalty Castle
Her first major independent magistrate mission. Adjudicating a three-clan contested claim to the Snow Plains mine, she ruled that all claims had lapsed through more than a century of non-exercise and that the property reverted to Imperial ownership. No clan lost to another clan; all three lost to the Imperial principle. It is the foundational framework of everything since.
The Fracture
Court intrigue at the City of the Rich Frog damaged the party's credibility. Monbon was publicly scarred in a duel and dismissed shortly afterward; the party split, and Setsuna travelled east with Mia.
The Midnight Treaty
She authored the Lion–Unicorn nonaggression treaty at the besieged castle gate, and reclaimed Monbon from captivity via the ransom invoice. The treaty staked ten honor per signatory, and held. Famously Successful was acquired here, at Glory 65.
The Assassination Attempt
She walked unarmed into the tent where assassins held Mia at knife-point, disarmed the principal attacker with Coiling Serpent Style, and directed Mia to strike. Mia survived, but lost her sight. Setsuna was severely wounded.
The Spear-Forest Ultimatum
She delivered seppuku-or-cooperation to both generals, standing between their armies — an Air-stance Command check in formal-lacquer register. She was compromised by the scene's end. Both generals pledged.
Recently
School Rank 3; Hidden in Smoke, Legal Scholarship, Paragon of Courage, and a major honor award. Honor 66 was secured through demonstrated action under fear, and Paragon of Courage acquired on the same basis. A further glory award is owed for the Spear-Forest Ultimatum, deferred to a later scene of public recognition.
Concealments

What follows is private to Setsuna. It is known to no one at court, and to only four people alive.

Approximately four months before the chronicle began, Setsuna took an Imperial-family lover at court in Otosan Uchi and conceived, during a period when her husband was on campaign in Unicorn lands. She departed for her assignment carrying the pregnancy and did not inform the father, who has never known and whom she does not pursue — a decision taken partly to protect him from legal exposure and partly to spare him knowledge that would mark him. She carried it through the Loyalty Castle mission and the early sessions, considering carrying to term seriously, before electing termination. Shiba Midori brewed the abortifacient and conducted the procedure; Kakita Kazume provided discreet logistical support. Setsuna was severely wounded in Void, and unmasked herself privately with Midori afterward.

The jeopardy is layered. Adultery is class-internal and framework-managed by her mother's instruction, though it would damage the marriage and her clan position if known. A concealed Imperial-family bloodline would have constituted bloodline fraud against the Imperial families had the child been claimed as Harunobu's. The unauthorised destruction of an Imperial bloodline — the termination itself, taken without Imperial sanction — is a capital crime against the throne.

Midori knows the procedure and the medical detail; Kazume deduced the existence of both; Aika and Ishika witnessed the bodily evidence. None of them knows the father's identity. Neither Aika nor Ishika has ever been formally asked for confidentiality — the trust is presumed at the deepest structural level, since any request for silence would itself constitute the breach. They serve under the knowledge that disclosure would carry capital exposure to all parties, themselves included, and they have continued to serve. Her husband knows nothing and must never know; learning of the infidelity would destroy him. A false rumour of a liaison with the Governor of the City of the Rich Frog operates as decoy noise.

The procedure left a severe Void wound that has persisted across the whole arc of the chronicle, partially healed and never fully restored. It raises the TN of her Void-stance and Void-skill checks rather than reducing her capacity, and it stands on her sheet still. It is at once the procedural cost and the integrated weight of the secret as carried in her body.

Trajectory

The Toshi Ranbo treaty is now her explicit ninjō: a trilateral settlement returning the city to Crane administration while binding the Lion and Unicorn into a stable peace. It arranges Emika's marriage into the Matsu to satisfy the grandmother's restoration demand, secures Harunobu's freedom through its prisoner-exchange clauses, and opens Crane–Unicorn trade. Its legal framework will be Imperial-grounded, consistent with her career-long doctrine.

Her build is consolidating toward the endgame that serves it. At school rank 5 the Formal Tea Ceremony binds agreements at the ceremonial register; at rank 6, Post Facto institutionalises the loophole-finder's instinct as a permanent capability — once per session, a legal precedent that retroactively justifies her own or her allies' actions. The title she is playing toward is Esteemed Negotiator, whose Calming Words is the doctrinal inverse of her school's Procedure and Protocol: where the school weaponises strife as pressure, the title clears strife to land fragile agreements.

Against that she has accumulated a population of clan-level operators with specific grievances — Noritomo and his allies among the Magistrates, the Lion and Unicorn families who lost the Snow Plains ruling, the Scorpion lord she invoiced, Mirumoto Kyou and Kakita Yoshitada. The signing of the Toshi Ranbo treaty will focus and intensify all of it, and her endgame defences are being built specifically for that convergence.

From the twentieth question, asked at her creation:

Setsuna should die unexpectedly and suddenly, so that she does not have a chance to shrink from it or overthink it. It is unlikely that her destiny is fulfilled in this life.

The player walked in believing the character would not complete her work. The Toshi Ranbo project, the grandmother's full restoration, the doctrine's full execution are generational tasks. What she completes will be partial; what she leaves unfinished will be substantial. The doctrine is the framework for working well under that condition.
