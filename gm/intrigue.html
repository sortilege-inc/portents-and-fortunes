Objectives

Difficulty is a momentum threshold and demeanor never touches it. Picking a type and a target proposes the difficulty the rules use; overwrite it freely. Click a pip to set the count; click the lit pip again to step back.

Persuade target numbers

TN of a Persuade check equals the target's vigilance, moved by their demeanor and by the skill/status discount, and never below 1. Toggle a demeanor off to see the unmodified number.

NOTE The −1 skill column is the Persuade discount: Courtesy when every target's status rank is higher than the speaker's, Command when every target's is lower, Games / Performance / other when equal.
