Objectives, momentum, and the target numbers as the demeanors move them.

Momentum and toggles are saved in this browser.

Objectives

Difficulty is a momentum threshold and demeanor never touches it. Click a pip to set the count; click the lit pip again to step back one.

Persuade target numbers

TN of a Persuade check equals the target's vigilance, moved by their demeanor and by the skill/status discount, and never below 1. Toggle a demeanor off to see the unmodified number.

NOTE The −1 skill column is the Persuade discount: Courtesy when every target's status rank is higher than the speaker's, Command when every target's is lower, Games / Performance / other when equal. Sadao is status 47 (rank 4) and outranks everyone here; the elders are rank 0.

NOTE Norikage's row sits at vigilance 1 until Earth Needs No Eyes is up — a Support action on a TN 1 Meditation (Earth) check, raising vigilance by his Earth Ring to 4. Toggle it to see the room's difficulty in reading him change.
