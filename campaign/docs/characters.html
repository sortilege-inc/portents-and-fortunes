Togashi Norikage

Togashi Tattooed Order · Monk · Rank 1

Born the winter the mountain fell silent, and marked from the cradle by a prophecy he did not ask for and is afraid was accurate. A theologian of the spider-marked ki, perceptive only when he stops moving, sent east with a village being resettled to watch what happens to it.
