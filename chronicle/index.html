The Record of Play

Chronicle

Session by session — the tale as it is told at the table.

The mountain has slept for twenty years. The first tremor has not yet been written.

The chronicle has not yet begun. Togashi Norikage has been made — his rings cast, his tattoo chosen, his abbot's charge received — and stands at the edge of his posting. When the first session is played, its record will appear here.

Each entry will hold the date, a short recap, and the moments that mattered — the omens read, the shrines found wanting, the truths spoken when a lie would have been easier.

Until then: read who he is, walk the Dragon lands, or — if you are the Master — turn to Behind the Veil.
