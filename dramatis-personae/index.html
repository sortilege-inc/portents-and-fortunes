The Cast

Dramatis Personae

Those already known to Norikage as the road begins. More will be added as they are met.

The Abbot

Togashi · Norikage's lord and master of his temple.

A Togashi abbot of the Tattooed Order, and the authority to whom Norikage answers. It is the abbot who gave the charge that sets this chronicle in motion: to go down into a shrinking village and watch — quietly — the samurai who are emptying it, and to report what he sees back to the temple. His name, his age, and his reasons are not yet known.

Status: named but unmet · Norikage's giri

Yogo Kenzan · "Brother Kenzan"

Brotherhood of Shinsei · the teacher from whom Norikage learned the most.

A monk of the Brotherhood of Shinsei, Scorpion-born under the Yogo name, and the person from whom Norikage says he learned the most. Their conversations shaped how the young Togashi thinks about faith and doubt. Where Kenzan is now, and what he believes, are threads yet to be drawn.

Status: known · mentor

Togashi Oharu

Provincial daimyō · of the regional capital.

A Togashi daimyō who governs from the regional capital. Norikage's original charge named Oharu as the one he served as "eyes and ears"; that posting has since shifted to the temple and its abbot, but the daimyō's administration remains a power over the lands he now walks.

Status: named · authority

The Priest of the Cradle

The one who read the omen — and gave the Jade Tadpole.

The priest who attended Norikage's birth, read the sign upon the silent child, and spoke the prophecy that has hung over him ever since. It was this priest who gave him the Jade Tadpole he still carries. Twenty years of retelling have blurred the exact words that were spoken; recovering them may be worth the doing.

Status: from the past · unresolved

The stranger, the administrator, the villagers — these will be written here as Norikage meets them.
